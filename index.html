Our story

From Dough to Fire

FORNO was born from a simple obsession: the perfect pizza. Our founder Marco spent three years learning the craft in Naples before opening our first kitchen in 2012.

Every batch of dough slow-ferments for 48 hours. Every sauce is hand-crushed from San Marzano tomatoes. Every topping is added with intention. We believe pizza is not fast food — it's craft food.
